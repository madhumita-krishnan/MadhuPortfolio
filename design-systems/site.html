Identity manual & design system

How M--K is made

The brand is terracotta on warm cream: fired-clay rust, sage stems, deep teal-green, and Madhu's own handwriting — all of it sampled out of one watercolour reference, color pallete I like.png. This document is written to be executable: every rule carries its exact value, so a person or an AI assistant can implement it without judgment calls.

The three governing principles

PrincipleWhat it means in practice
The watercolour lives in the artwork, not the controlsArt is painterly (blooms, paint-wash, the pot); UI is flat. A control is a solid fill or a flat 1.5px outline and nothing else — no glass, no texture, no shadow, no gradient on anything clickable. Exceptions are named and closed: the play icon, the nav bar, and the day/night toggle.
Sampled, not styledPalette values are taken from the reference image's pixels, not chosen by eye — and from the top-decile-chroma pixels of each region, not the mean, because averaging a watercolour region always greys it out. Any retuning starts from the reference, never from a colour picker.
One system, two lightsEvery colour has a day value and a night value; night is the same palette moved onto its deep-green surface with rust/teal resampled lighter so every text pairing keeps WCAG AA. Nothing is designed for one mode only.

The hand

Her handwriting is a real typeface, and it is the brand's voice.

RuleSpecification
The faceMadhu Hand — cut from her own written sheets by tools/make-hand-font.js. Self-hosted (assets/fonts/madhu-hand.woff, ~14KB, font-display: swap), fallback ui-rounded, cursive. The .ttf ships beside it for download/Figma use but is never listed as a src.
Where it appearsExactly two places: (1) the hero sentence — the one paragraph of the site set in it, clamp(1.18rem, 1.78vw, 1.52rem), line-height 1.75 (her descenders reach 64% of the em; tighter and a g's tail lands in the next line), max measure 860px / 75 characters; (2) the hover face of every control (see Interaction states).
NeverBody copy, headings, captions, labels at rest. The hand is a voice, not a text face.
Metrics constantOverlaying the hand on a sans label uses top: 50%; transform: translateY(-.52em) — the −0.52em constant folds both faces' metrics and scales with type size. Use it verbatim for any new hand-swap.
No digitsThe face has no numerals. Copy set in the hand must be rewritten to avoid digits — never faux-fill with another font. (The "!" exists, composed from her l and full stop.)
Its colourAlways --ink-blue on light grounds (the wordmark blue of her reference sheet); on fills it takes the fill's label colour.

Colour treatment

RuleSpecification
Display type is blueAll display headings (Cormorant Garamond) set in --ink-blue #2A6B80 — the way the wordmark is blue on her reference sheet. Body copy stays the warm near-black --text-primary #26332F so long paragraphs read easily.
Terracotta is for action--accent #B04516 marks things to do: primary buttons, eyebrows, links, the active tab, the cursor dot. It is not a decoration colour.
Teal is for quiet--teal-baby #47899A carries tertiary controls, chips, the logo wall. The invariant is 3:1 contrast on cream, not the hex — if cream changes, resample the teal to hold 3:1.
The paper is load-bearing--bg-base #F5EAD3 is the exact paper of the reference image. The hero blooms are cut out of that photograph, so the cream showing through gaps inside a petal is the page. Shift this token and the bloom gaps stop matching.
Deep green is the stage--bg-deep #1E3634 grounds case-study heroes, project cards and the night surface. Cream inks on it; never terracotta outlines.
One watch-outRust type on a rust petal is the one pairing on this site with no contrast at all — the hero eyebrow must never land on the solid middle of a bloom.

Imagery

RuleSpecification
The blooms are literalHero art is cut pixel-for-pixel out of color pallete I like.png by tools/lift-flowers.js. Never generate, redraw or "recreate" flower art — every drawn attempt was rejected. To change the art, move the crop rectangles in CUTS.
No decorative gradientsHer instruction: "don't use gradients — think more paint strokes texture." Large surfaces are flat palette tokens with her own brush swatch masked over them (--accent-wash through paint-wash.png, alpha-only). Gradients survive only as materials: the glass rim, the titanium phone rail, browser chrome, and the legibility scrim over photos.
GrainA fixed fractal-noise layer at opacity: .045 sits over everything (z-index 60) — paper, not effect.
Device framesPhones and browser windows are pure CSS, no bezel images. Phone chrome is sized in cqw (1% of device width); the browser in --u = 1 logical px of a 1280pt window (40 tab strip, 34 tabs, 12 traffic lights, 40 toolbar, 10 window radius). Feed phones un-framed screens; within 5% of 9:19.5 they stretch, beyond that they cover-crop from the top.
Delight is drawn, then codedThe stick figure who lives on the hero type, the koi leaping from the pot, the dog — all originate as her drawings/objects and are lifted (alpha-from-graphite, gamma 0.55 for the dog), never illustrated by software. Same rule as the blooms.
Media weightVideos are compressed MP4s under ~5MB with poster frames, preload="none"; images ship width/height or aspect-ratio and loading="lazy". Nothing downloads until needed.

Do & don't

Do
  • Set every button label in CAPITALS, and give every control the handwriting hover
  • Keep display type in ink-blue and body in the warm near-black
  • Use the deep-green band for case-study heroes, with cream inks on it
  • Run every user-facing string through typo() so no word sits alone on a last line
  • Check both modes: every change must hold in day and night
  • Keep captions at ≥ 12pt with AA contrast (--text-caption exists for this)
Don't
  • Don't generate or redraw the flower art — the blooms are cutouts of her painting
  • Don't add decorative gradients, hover shimmers/sweeps, or drop shadows on controls
  • Don't use arrow glyphs in UI copy or controls — a link grows its gap instead
  • Don't put glass on anything except the nav, the day/night toggle, and the four company chips
  • Don't write headings that run past two lines, end in periods, or hold two sentences
  • Don't blur the whole hero (rejected — the blur belongs to the eyebrow treatment only)
  • Don't set digits in Madhu Hand — the face has none

Contrast contracts, not suggestions: accent on cream = 4.74:1 (AA text). Baby teal on cream = 3:1 — that ratio is the invariant, not the hex. Night pairs were verified in-session: all body text ≥ 4.5:1, display/UI ≥ 3:1. --text-on-accent is cream by day and flips dark (#1D130B) at night because night's accent fills are lighter.
Shadows are tokens too: shadow-soft rgba(30,54,52,.12) · shadow-mid .28 · shadow-strong .42 · shadow-clay rgba(124,45,14,.38). At night all four go to black-based values (.35/.5/.65/.55).

Composition rules

RuleSpecification
Never a widowEvery rendered string passes through typo(), which ties the last two words in <span class="nw"> (white-space: nowrap); text-wrap: pretty on all text elements handles the rest. Use typo() for prose, esc() only for attributes and single words.
HeadingsMaximum two lines at any viewport (build enforces with per-string width checks); a heading that fits one line stays one line. No periods at the end, never two sentences.
Hand measureSentences in the hand stay under 75 characters per line; each sentence starts its own line (.hs blocks, 0.5em between) but the block reads as one paragraph.

The liquid glass material

Four things make glass read as glass — the full recipe, exactly:

LayerExact value
1 · It bends the scenebackdrop-filter: url(#glassWarp) blur(20px) saturate(1.5) — an SVG low-frequency feDisplacementMap, so edges behind the panel visibly shift. The warp list is closed at six elements: the nav, the four company chips, the day/night toggle. Do not promote it to all glass.
2 · Filllinear-gradient(150deg, rgba(255,255,255,.50) 0%, rgba(255,252,244,.42) 52%, rgba(255,255,255,.26) 100%) + border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,.60)
3 · Lightinset crescents, not a 1px line: inset 0 1.5px 1.5px -1px rgba(255,255,255,.98) (top), inset 0 -2px 2px -1px …,.62 (bottom), inset ±1.5px 0 2px -1px …,.42 (sides), glow inset 0 0 18px …,.16, drop 0 10px 30px var(--shadow-soft)
4 · Prism rima conic rainbow masked to a 1.5px edge ring, mix-blend-mode: screen, opacity .9 (night .45): stops at 22° rgba(255,120,128,.34), 42° rgba(255,214,96,.30), 62° rgba(126,240,186,.26), 82° rgba(112,178,255,.34), 102° rgba(192,152,255,.24)
Night valuesfill rgba(24,36,33,.46), border rgba(255,255,255,.18), crescents .35/.20/.14, glow .06
BannedNo sweeping hover shimmer, ever — it reads as a swipe effect, not a material.
Performance rule with a scar: an animated feTurbulence inside a backdrop-filter once cost 17fps while idle. Glass filters are static; nothing inside a backdrop-filter animates.

Motion rules

RuleSpecification
Reduced motionprefers-reduced-motion disables the cursor, the theme animation, reveals and the creatures — plus a fail-safe that forces all content visible even if animations never run. On iOS, Reduce Motion also blocks video autoplay: treat "everything broken" reports as this first.
AutoplayPanel clips always autoplay (muted, looped, in-view). Low Power Mode vetoes play() — retry in-view clips on touchend. Videos pause off-screen.
Nothing floatsFlat controls never drift or bob (contradicts "solid fills only"). The only idle motion is the glass float and the creatures.
Delight is physicalThe koi's leap is a true projectile arc out of the pot (every pot click re-launches); the stick figure grips real glyph outlines, feet snap-planted — never floating, no fake holds. New characters follow the same law: real physics, real anchors.

Iconography

RuleSpecification

The play icon▶ inside .btn.icon.solid (56px circle, terracotta fill, cream glyph) — the one icon allowed on media, and one of the named exceptions to "controls are flat fills only".

Interaction states

Every control has all five states, and every control hover-swaps into the hand. Try these — they are live.

default · hover (hand) · active · disabled · focus (Tab)
StateSolid (primary)Outline (secondary)Teal (tertiary)
Defaultfill accent · label text-on-accent1.5px accent line · accent label1.5px teal-baby line · ink-blue label

Focusoutline 2px ink-blue, offset 3px — one ring for every control on the site, teal so it never disappears against a terracotta fill
The hand-swap, exactly: data-hand carries the label already capitalised. On hover/focus the sans label goes color: transparent and a ::after in Madhu Hand fades in over it — font-size: 1.05em, positioned top: 50%; translateY(-.52em), opacity 180ms. The overlay is absolute so the pill never changes width. Hover-only media query: touch devices keep the sans label.
Contrast decisions baked into the states: the teal button's hover fill is ink-blue, not baby teal (cream on baby teal is 3.6:1; on ink-blue 5.4:1). The outline hover fills with accent-press, not accent, because the cream label needs the darker step. Do not "simplify" these.

Design patterns

PatternSpecification
The floating chromeTwo fixed glass pills and only two: the nav (top-center, 14px inset, max 820px, radius 999) and the day/night toggle (bottom-left, 18px inset). Both wear glass + warp because they float over scrolling content — a flat fill pinned over moving content reads as a sticker. Everything else that looks like chrome is in-flow and flat.
Night modeSun-timed: the boot script in <head> stamps data-theme on <html> before first paint — stored choice first, otherwise the viewer's own sunrise/sunset. The toggle's label names the mode you'll get ("Night Mode" by day), rim is a hairline at half strength matching its label ink (day: rgba(176,69,22,.5) + accent label; night: rgba(133,196,214,.55) + ink-blue label).
The cursorFine pointers only, motion allowed only: a 13px solid dot (accent by day, ink-blue by night, shadow rgba(38,51,47,.22)). Over a case-study card it flips three times (rotateY 0→1080°, .5s) then rolls out into a 140×34px pill reading "I'M CURIOUS" (.62rem caps, bg-base ink). First Tab returns the real cursor.
Phone edge fadesiOS-style progressive blur strips top and bottom on phones: three stacked backdrop layers per strip — blur 18px at the screen edge, 8px to ~60% of the strip, 3px full height — every mask a smoothstep so no fade has a findable start. z-index 40: under the nav (50) and toggle (80), so the pills float on softened ground.
Case-study readingOne section at a time: each .cs-section (label + heading + body + evidence) crossfades as a unit on scroll. Headlines state the business problem, not the product question. Structure: hero band → intro → meta → stats band → sections → reflection → next-project card.
Before/afterComparisons are labeled columns ("Before" / "After — tested winners"); phone pairs are labeled "Spotify today" / "Redesign". Header and intro must not repeat each other.
Content pipelineAll copy lives in data/*.json ("the database" — there is no backend, ever). The build compiles JSON → static HTML; every string passes typo(). QA: append ?shot=1200 to any URL for a settled, fully-revealed render at 1200px scroll.
Compatibility floorNo regex lookbehind in shipped JS (one literal once killed all of app.js on iOS < 16.4). Feature-detect, never UA-sniff. The site must boot with JS disabled — reveals fail open.
Monitoringtools/site-check.js runs daily (6am ET GitHub Action + cloud routine): checks the live domain, tokens, and media health.
